Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short: Pagewise has no server and no analytics. The developer never receives any of your data. Your API keys, profile, skills and logs stay on your computer. Page content and your questions go only to the AI provider whose key you added (Google, or Groq if you add a backup key), and only when you use a feature.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the Pagewise Chrome extension and this website. Pagewise is open source; you can read exactly what it does in its source code.

What Pagewise keeps on your computer

Stored in Chrome's local extension storage on this computer, never synced to your Google account, and never sent to the developer:

  • Your API keys for Gemini and, optionally, Groq.
  • Settings, such as your language and tone.
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: name, email, phone, college, degree, graduation year, skills, links and an "about" note.
  • Skills you create or import.
  • Agent logs: the steps of your last 20 agent tasks and what you decided for each.
  • Safety lists: sites you blocked, sites you added as your own, and private sites where you agreed to send selected text.

Page-chat history and cached summaries are kept only until you close Chrome.

What leaves your computer, and where it goes

Only when you use a feature, Pagewise sends what that feature needs to the AI provider:

  • Google Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), and
  • Groq API (api.groq.com), only if you added a Groq key, as a backup for text features.

Depending on the feature, that can include the page's text, its title and address, text you selected, a screenshot you captured, your question, a list of the page's buttons and fields (for the agent and Find), YouTube links you asked about, and the text of a skill you ran. Profile values are sent only for a drafted reply (your name and preferred tone) or a skill that uses them, and the panel shows a "Profile used" chip when that happens. When the agent fills a form from your profile, the AI is told only the field names, such as "name" or "email". It never gets the values to choose from. The values are typed into the page after you approve each step, and from then on they're part of the page the agent reads on its next step, as anything else in a form would be. Your API key is sent only to its own provider, to sign the request.

What happens there is governed by that provider's terms: Gemini API terms and Google's privacy policy; Groq's privacy policy. Google's terms for the free Gemini tier allow it to use what you send to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Don't use Pagewise on content you wouldn't share on those terms.

What Pagewise removes or refuses to send

  • Before any text is sent, card numbers, Aadhaar numbers, PAN, one-time codes, UPI IDs and API keys are replaced with placeholders such as [card number removed]. You can also hide emails and phone numbers in Settings → Safety.
  • On banking, payment and checkout pages, Pagewise sends nothing at all.
  • On private pages (email, documents, messaging, ID and health portals, and pages with a login form), it never reads the page. If you agree once for that site, it sends only the text you select, without the surrounding page.
  • It never reads or types into password, OTP or card fields.

What Pagewise never does

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking, in the extension or on this website.
  • No Pagewise server: the developer can't see your data, because it's never sent to them.
  • Your data is never sold, shared with third parties other than the AI provider you chose, or used for anything other than the feature you asked for. It isn't used to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

The use of information received through Pagewise adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Settings → Clear all my data deletes your profile, skills, agent logs, chat history and API keys from this computer.
  • You can remove a key, edit or delete your profile and skills, block sites, and revoke private-site permissions at any time in Settings.
  • Removing the extension deletes everything it stored. To ask Google or Groq about data they received, contact them under their own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date, and noted in the changelog.
