सहायक · a helper in your browser

Understand any page. Act on it only when you say so.

Sahayak lives in Chrome's side panel. It summarises articles and YouTube videos in English or Hinglish, answers questions from the page itself, reads what's on your screen, and fills forms one step at a time, waiting for your Approve before every single action.

Read · See · Act

📖

Read

A TL;DR, five key points and next steps for any article, and chapter-by-chapter notes for public YouTube videos. Then ask follow-up questions: answers come only from the page, and Sahayak tells you when the page doesn't say.

Right-click any selection to Explain, Translate or Draft a reply.

🖼️

See

Charts, tables, diagrams, anything on screen. Capture it, crop to the part you mean, and ask.

Find it for me points at the button or field you describe, without touching anything.

✋

Act, with you in control

Give it a task like "fill this application from my profile". Sahayak proposes one step at a time, shows the exact value and why, and waits. You Approve, Skip or Stop.

Submit, send and delete need a second confirmation. Every step is logged.

Also: a local profile for forms and email sign-offs, one-click skills such as "Exam notes" that you can share as a small file, and a free Groq backup for when Gemini's free tier is busy.

The safety promise

These rules are built into Sahayak's code. They don't depend on the AI behaving.

  • Nothing happens without your Approve. One step at a time, and a second confirmation for anything that submits, sends or deletes.
  • No banking, payments or UPI. Sahayak switches off completely on those pages, and there's no override. 🔴
  • Private pages stay private. Email, documents and messages are never read. You can still send text you select, after agreeing once per site. 🟡
  • No passwords, OTPs or card numbers. Sahayak never types them and never presses pay, checkout or log in.
  • Sensitive numbers are removed from everything sent to the AI: cards, Aadhaar, PAN, OTPs and UPI IDs.
  • Your data stays on your computer. Key, profile, skills and logs live in Chrome's local storage. No Sahayak server, no analytics. One click deletes it all.

Install in four steps

  1. Download

    Get sahayak-vX.Y.Z.zip from the latest release.

  2. Unzip

    Into a folder you'll keep. Chrome loads Sahayak from it.

  3. Developer mode

    Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode, top right.

  4. Load unpacked

    Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder, the one with manifest.json.

Then click the Sahayak icon, open Settings, and paste a free key from Google AI Studio. Needs Chrome 116 or later. The README walks through the first run.

Try the agent safely

The practice site has fake pages to try Sahayak's agent on: an internship application, a shop, a club directory, and login, OTP, card and UPI pages that Sahayak must refuse. Nothing you type there goes anywhere.
